About Breakline

Better forecasts.
Better sessions.

Breakline is a surf-planning app designed to make ocean conditions easier to understand—so surfers can choose the right spot, time their session, and arrive with confidence.

Our purpose

The ocean is complex.
Your forecast shouldn’t be.

Surf forecasts often scatter the information that matters across dense charts and separate data sources. Breakline brings swell height and direction, wind, tide, and timing into a single spot-specific outlook.

Our goal is not to replace a surfer’s judgment. It is to give that judgment a clearer starting point—whether you are planning a dawn patrol, learning your local break, or chasing the best window on a day off.

What guides us

01

Clarity over clutter

We translate raw conditions into a forecast that is useful at a glance.

02

Local context matters

Every break responds differently. Forecasts should reflect the place.

03

Respect the ocean

Conditions change quickly. We encourage awareness, preparation, and sound judgment.
